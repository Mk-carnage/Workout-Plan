Complete Rest

Testosterone is Built During Recovery

🌟 Today's Mission

😴 Sleep 7–9 hours — peak T production happens at night
🥩 Protein + healthy fats (eggs, meat, avocado, nuts)
☀️ Sunlight — boosts Vitamin D → boosts testosterone
🧘 Keep stress low — cortisol directly kills testosterone
🚫 No workout — complete muscle repair day
REST = GROWTH
Muscles don't grow in the gym — they grow when you rest, eat well, and sleep.
📈 Progression Protocol

Week 1–2: Volume

Learn the movements. Add 1–2 reps per exercise before adding weight.

Week 3–4: Load

At top rep range, add 2.5–5 kg. Never jump more than 5 kg at once.

Slow the reps

3 sec on the way down. More time under tension = more muscle signal.

Track it

Write down weight + reps every session. If you don't track, you won't progress.

🍽 Nutrition Timing

Pre-Workout

🍌 Banana + water (or rice + dal if time allows)
☕ Black coffee 30 min before — natural focus boost
⏱ Eat 30–60 min before

Post-Workout

🥚 3–4 eggs or chicken / paneer
🍚 Rice or chapati — refuel glycogen
⏱ Eat within 45 min after session
